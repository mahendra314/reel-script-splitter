Zyadatar log video ke pehle do second mein hi scroll kar dete hain. Isliye aapki opening line ka ek hi kaam hai: dekhne wale ko ruk kar dekhne ki wajah dena.

Result se shuru karo, setup se nahi. Batao ki agar viewer end tak dekhega to use kya milega.

Apne sentences chhote rakho. Chhota sentence sunne mein aasan hota hai, screen par padhne mein aasan hota hai, aur tez bolne par bhi samajh aata hai.

Har pause jo zaroori nahi hai, use kaat do. Silence tab theek hai jab wo tension banaye, lekin khali jagah se log aksar chale jaate hain.

Ek clip mein ek hi idea rakho. Agar pandrah second mein teen cheezein sikhane ki koshish karoge, to koi bhi cheez dimaag mein nahi baithegi.

Ant mein ek saaf next step do. Viewer ko batao kya karna hai: part two ke liye follow karo, is video ko baad ke liye save karo, ya apna jawab comment karo.

Record karne se pehle script ko zor se bolkar test karo. Agar koi sentence ek saans mein bolna mushkil lage, to use do hisson mein tod do.
